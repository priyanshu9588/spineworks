## Visual system

Original SVG drawings combine ivory engraved contours, fine anatomical detail, sparse construction marks, and mint paths on pine. Each composition follows its own mechanism: a scan, feedback loop, continuous tract, range diagram, or state transition. The chapter names form a readable story: see, act, keep, choose, update.

All five studies use the same natural 720 × 420 SVG viewport. Their evidence is real HTML. Phones retain the persona and evidence while suppressing small drawing annotations. A stable drawing and evidence footprint keeps scroll selection predictable; short windows and enlarged text return all five studies to document flow. Native scrolling selects each chapter. The existing animated ASCII hero and dissolving introduction remain.

## Motion direction

Each 7.2-second cycle animates the mechanism inside a complete, steady drawing, then leaves a reading hold. Ivory signal heads and mint tails share one visual treatment; each anatomy has its own choreography:

- The eye receives the page signal, focuses its pupil, illuminates iris fibres, and sends the interpreted control outward.
- The hand articulates one finger as intent arrives; the result travels around a separate return path.
- The spinal tract carries a signal through sequential nerve roots while its continuous central connection stays visible.
- The joint reaches through its available range. A separate blocked branch keeps the unavailable action explicit.
- Dendrite signals converge in the neuron, travel through the axon and myelin, cross the synapse, and highlight the disabled-to-enabled graph.

Motion begins after the loader exits and at least 60% of the drawing is visible. A shared pause control freezes the current frame and retains that preference across chapters and responsive layouts. Offscreen and hidden-document studies pause. Reduced motion and JavaScript disabled retain the complete static illustrations. CSS animates internal SVG paths and groups without changing the stage dimensions or moving the explanatory text.

## Reference study

[Modal](https://modal.com/) pairs a consistent material treatment with literal code and runtime evidence. [Supermemory](https://supermemory.ai/) separates its expressive brand imagery from clear mechanism diagrams; timed inspection of its circuit illustration showed sparse traveling signals over a steady, intricate structure. [Linear](https://linear.app/) composes each feature illustration around its operation: intake and planning have different visual structures within one consistent system. Spine adopts those principles with its own biological theme and original drawings; no competitor artwork or code is included.

Gray's Anatomy eye, hand, and brain plates were evaluated as public-domain anatomical references. The shipped studies are original SVG constructions, not the downloaded scans.
